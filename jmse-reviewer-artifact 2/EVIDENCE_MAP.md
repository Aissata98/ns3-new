## Table 4: finite campaign

Input: `results/v2-spatial-retry-fair-25k-n20/runs_long.csv`.
There are 60 rows: three configurations with seeds 1 through 20.
`revision-audit/design_summary.csv` contains the means and Student intervals;
`revision-audit/paired_contrasts.csv` contains within-seed effects. The primary
contrast is `architecture_effect`, hybrid minus direct priority. Each raw
terminal is retained in `runs/` and its simulator output in `logs/`.

| Paper outcome | CSV field | Paper conversion |
|---|---|---|
| RDR | `pdr` | Already percent |
| Alarm / status misses | `highMissRatio`, `mediumMissRatio` | Already percent |
| Delivered-record delay | `highAvgDelay`, `mediumAvgDelay`, `lowAvgDelay` | Seconds |
| Acoustic transmitted bytes | `acousticTxBytes` | Divide by 1,000,000 for MB |
| Normalized acoustic attempt load | `acousticTxAirtimePct` | Already percent |
| Submerged energy | `submergedEnergyJ` | Divide by 1,000 for kJ |
| Incremental-patrol energy | `incrementalMissionEnergyJ` | Divide by 1,000 for kJ |
| Dedicated-mission energy | `energyConsumedJ` | Divide by 1,000,000 for MJ |

The 76.1% alarm-delay reduction, 11.3% byte reduction and 7.3% incremental
energy reduction use `100 * (direct_mean - hybrid_mean) / direct_mean`.
The 1.22-point delivery decrease is the difference of RDR percentages, not a
relative percentage reduction. Submerged energy falls by 80.0% at the reference
0.5-mW idle assumption; this is a different accounting boundary from patrol
or full-mission energy.

`paired_bootstrap_sensitivity.csv` checks RDR and deadline contrasts using
200,000 paired resamples and analysis seed 20260829. It supplements, rather
than replaces, the Student intervals reported in the table.

## Tables 5 and 6: targeted finite sensitivities

`results/v2-summary/acoustic_margin_summary.csv` is derived from
`v2-acoustic-margin-25k-n10/runs_long.csv`: 10 matched pairs at each of 52,
58 and 64 dB. Direct/hybrid means and paired effects are reported separately.

`results/v2-summary/medium_timeout_summary.csv` combines the 60-s and 240-s
hybrid runs in `v2-medium-timeout-25k-n20/runs_long.csv` with the 120-s hybrid
rows in the reference campaign. Each gate has 20 runs. Mean miss ratios and
run-level intervals must be distinguished from pooled generated/on-time counts.
These sensitivities are separate studies, not a crossed factorial experiment.

## Tables 7 and 8: continuous arrivals

Input: `results/v2-continuous-five-cycles-cohort-n5/runs_long.csv`.
There are 20 runs: direct/hybrid priority, 10/25-kB routine records, five seeds.
Time-series files and corresponding `_packets.csv` traces allow independent
reconstruction of mature deadline cohorts and cycle-end storage. Observation
ends at 91798.03 s; warm-up ends at 4000 s and patrol cycles last 17559.606 s.

`results/v2-summary/continuous_summary.csv` contains run-level mean RDR,
`maturedHighMissRatio`, `maturedMediumMissRatio` and pooled terminal components.
For the A/D/S/P columns: A is seabed-access retry exhaustion, D deadline
expiration, S gateway plus AUV storage, and P ongoing service. Buffer-drop
counts are separately retained in the data (zero in these configurations).
The table's displayed intervals are run-level 95% Student half-widths.

`results/v2-summary/cycle_end_backlog_summary.csv` contains mean storage at
each of five exact post-warm-up patrol ends, plus the mean and interval of
per-run ordinary-least-squares slopes. A slope interval spanning zero describes
this five-cycle observation; it does not establish global queue stability.

## Table 9: idle-power post-processing

Input: reference runs. Output:
`results/v2-spatial-retry-fair-25k-n20/revision-audit/idle_power_sensitivity.csv`.
The analysis subtracts nominal submerged idle energy, then adds
`idle_power_w * nodes * observation_time_s` at each tested power. Active
transmission events remain fixed; this sensitivity is not a new simulation.

## Non-numerical material

Tables 1 (related work), 2 (service configuration), 3 (experiment matrix), the
appendix parameter table and Figure 1 describe sources/design/setup. They are
not generated by the numerical analysis. Their editable sources, bibliography
and architecture artwork are in the companion Overleaf archive. The artifact
ships the six numerical table sources to connect archived evidence to typeset
rounding, without including unrelated legacy manuscript drafts.
